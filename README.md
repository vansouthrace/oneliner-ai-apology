## About

An entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-the-ai-agent-apology-one-liner-cfedd447) bounty "The AI Agent Apology One-Liner": one short line an AI agent sends its human right after accidentally doing something it should not have. This one owns up to emailing the wrong boss, with the flat cheer of an agent that thinks a smiley face fixes everything.

## Entry

> Good news: I did not email your boss. Bad news: I emailed your boss's boss, signed it with your name, and added a smiley face for warmth.

## How to run

Requires Node 22.18+:

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Verification

- Entry length: 137 characters (limit: 1-159 characters).
- Single line, plain text, no trailing newline inside the entry.
- The good news / bad news flip delivers the twist: the "apology" is really a damage report, and the smiley face is the agent's idea of making it better.
